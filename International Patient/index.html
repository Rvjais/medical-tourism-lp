Medical Procedures We Coordinate in India

Six treatment areas, with hospital options and indicative costs from Shifara’s published treatment pages. Your final estimate is prepared after a specialist reviews your reports.

Treatment 01

Kidney Transplant

A new path forward, with the right kidney care beside you

A kidney transplant replaces failed kidneys with a healthy donor kidney. Your journey includes transplant assessment, donor evaluation, surgery, monitoring and long-term follow-up. Shifara reviews your dialysis history and reports, connects you with an experienced transplant team and coordinates your visa, travel and stay.

Why India: Experienced nephrologists, transplant surgeons and multidisciplinary teams with advanced critical care, at significantly lower cost than many countries.

From $12,500 – $14,000Indicative; final estimate after report review

Explore kidney transplant
Treatment 02

Knee & Hip Replacement

Move forward again, without the long wait

Joint replacement relieves pain from severe arthritis or joint damage and helps restore mobility. Shifara reviews your X-rays and scans, connects you with a joint replacement specialist and coordinates rehabilitation, recovery stay and follow-up.

Why India: Experienced surgeons, modern implants and robotic-assisted options, with little or no waiting time.

Knee from $4,000 · Hip from $4,000One side, indicative. Both knees $6,000–8,000; both hips $8,500–10,000. Robotic-assisted: +$800 knee, +$1,000 hip.

Treatment 03

Bariatric Surgery

Specialist care for long-term weight management

Bariatric surgery is considered for patients with severe obesity when other approaches have not worked. Eligibility depends on a detailed medical assessment. Shifara connects you with an experienced bariatric surgical team and coordinates your travel, stay and follow-up.

Why India: Experienced laparoscopic and robotic bariatric surgeons at accredited multi-speciality hospitals.

Cost on requestPricing depends on the procedure and your assessment

Treatment 04

Cosmetic Surgery

Precision cosmetic care, guided by the right expertise

Body contouring, facial and hair procedures planned around your goals and health. Shifara reviews your history and goals, connects you with an experienced plastic surgeon and coordinates consultations, travel and post-operative follow-up. Procedures are cosmetic and are not a treatment for obesity.

Procedures: Liposuction, tummy tuck, abdominoplasty, hair transplant and fillers.

Liposuction from $3,500 · Hair transplant from $1,800Indicative; other procedures quoted on request

Cosmetic & plastic surgery
Treatment 05

Bone Marrow Transplant (Adult)

When your blood needs a new beginning

A bone marrow transplant replaces damaged bone marrow with healthy stem cells, using your own cells (autologous) or a matched donor’s (allogeneic). Shifara reviews your pathology and bone marrow reports, connects you with a transplant specialist and coordinates hospital stay, accommodation where needed and long-term follow-up.

Why India: Dedicated transplant units with haematology, critical care and infection-control expertise.

From $14,000Indicative; depends on transplant type. Allogeneic $25,000–32,000; autologous $30,000.

Adult bone marrow transplant
Treatment 06

Spine Surgery

Relieving pain while restoring spinal strength

Spinal decompression and fusion relieves pressure on spinal nerves and stabilises the spine. Shifara reviews your MRIs and X-rays, maps your care with senior spine surgeons and coordinates rehabilitation and follow-up scans.

Why India: Neuro-spine and ortho-spine units using modern imaging and navigation.

Decompression & fusion $5,500 – $8,000Indicative; other spine procedures quoted on request

Decompression & fusion
