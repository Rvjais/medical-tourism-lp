Shifara reviews your medical records, coordinates transplant consultations and supports you with hospital admission, travel arrangements, accommodation and post-transplant follow-up.

General medical tourism FAQs

India offers a range of medical treatment options across multiple specialities, including cardiac care, oncology, orthopaedics, organ transplantation and fertility treatment. International patients can explore hospitals, specialist consultations and treatment estimates based on their medical requirements.

Finding the right hospital depends on your medical condition, treatment requirements, doctor availability and budget. Shifara Health helps international patients explore suitable hospitals, connect with relevant specialists and coordinate medical consultations in India.

International patients can share their medical reports, diagnostic results and treatment history for an initial review. Based on doctor availability, Shifara Health helps coordinate online medical consultations and treatment discussions before travel.

Treatment cost and affordability FAQs

The cost of medical treatment in India varies depending on the procedure, hospital, specialist, medical condition and length of stay. Patients can request personalised treatment estimates after sharing their medical reports and requirements.

Treatment costs in India may be lower than in several other countries, depending on the procedure and hospital. International patients can compare treatment estimates, understand what is included and review available medical care options before making a decision.

Medical visa and travel assistance FAQs

Foreign nationals travelling to India for medical treatment may be eligible for an Indian e-Medical Visa or another applicable visa category, subject to nationality and government requirements. Patients should check the latest eligibility criteria before applying.

Shifara Health can guide international patients through the medical travel process, including information about medical visa requirements, hospital documentation and travel coordination. Visa approval remains subject to the relevant Indian authorities.

Shifara Health can assist patients in coordinating travel-related requirements, including accommodation guidance, airport transfers and hospital visits, depending on the services available at the selected location.
