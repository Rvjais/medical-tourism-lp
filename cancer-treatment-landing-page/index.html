Medical Oncology

Medical oncology uses medicines and systemic therapies to treat cancer. Treatment may include chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy or hormone therapy, depending on cancer type, stage and molecular profile.

Key Options

  • Chemotherapy
  • Targeted therapy
  • Immunotherapy
  • Hormone therapy
  • Precision medicine
  • Molecular and genetic testing
  • Clinical trial access where appropriate
  • Supportive and palliative care
Request Medical Oncology Consultation

Surgical Oncology

Surgical oncology focuses on the surgical management of solid tumours. Depending on the cancer and your clinical needs, you may be evaluated for minimally invasive, robotic, organ-preserving or complex cancer surgery.

Key Options

  • Tumour removal
  • Minimally invasive surgery
  • Robotic cancer surgery
  • Organ-preserving surgery
  • Complex cancer surgery
  • Reconstructive procedures
  • Lymph node surgery
  • Multidisciplinary surgical planning
Find a Surgical Oncologist

Radiation Oncology

Radiation therapy uses precisely delivered radiation to treat cancer. It may be the main treatment or be combined with surgery and systemic therapy.

Key Options

  • IMRT
  • IGRT
  • SBRT
  • SRS
  • Brachytherapy
  • Precision radiation therapy
  • Image-guided treatment
  • Combined chemoradiation
Find a Radiation Oncologist

Interventional Oncology

Interventional oncology uses image-guided, minimally invasive procedures to diagnose or treat selected cancers.

Key Options

  • Image-guided tumour procedures
  • Tumour ablation
  • Liver-directed therapies
  • Biopsy procedures
  • Vascular interventions
  • Palliative interventions

Why Choose India for Cancer Treatment?

India is among the world’s most trusted medical tourism destinations for cancer care, welcoming hundreds of thousands of international patients every year. Here is why families around the world choose it for cancer treatment.

Experienced Cancer Specialists

Access oncologists across medical, surgical, radiation, haemato-oncology and sub-specialties, many with decades of experience.

Advanced Technology

Partner hospitals may offer robotic surgery, precision radiation, molecular diagnostics, immunotherapy and other advanced treatments. Availability varies by hospital.

Multidisciplinary Care

Complex cases can be reviewed jointly by oncologists, surgeons, radiologists, pathologists and other specialists.

Treatment That Costs Less

Most procedures cost 60–80% less than in the US, UK or Gulf, without compromising on quality.

Prompt Access

Specialist consultations and treatment are generally available without long waiting lists, which matters in time-sensitive cancer care.

Language and Cultural Comfort

English is widely spoken, and [Brand] arranges interpreters so you and your family can communicate clearly.
